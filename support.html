Support & Help

Welcome to STORIEE support. We're here to help you enjoy writing Stories with friends.

Contact support

For technical problems, account issues, feedback or safety concerns, email hairypeanut@mail.com.

Please include a short description of the issue and, if helpful, your device model and iOS version. Never send passwords or authentication codes.

What is STORIEE?

STORIEE is a collaborative storytelling game. Friends take turns adding up to 100 words to a Story. The same player cannot take two consecutive turns.

Signing in

STORIEE uses Sign in with Apple. Sign in with the same Apple account to return to your player profile.

Friends and favourites

When you join a shared Story, you and its other participants are automatically added as friends. Open Friends to see your connections, tap the heart to mark a favourite, or choose Edit Friends to remove someone.

Removing a friendship does not remove existing Story contributions.

Starting a Story and inviting friends

Choose Start Story, select friends to invite and set a registration window of 15 minutes, 1 hour or 5 hours.

Friends can accept while the invitation is valid. The Story can begin as soon as a second player joins; accepted players can keep writing after the registration window closes.

You can also use a Story invitation code where supported.

Stories and contributions

Each turn permits up to 100 words, with no daily contribution limit. A writing claim lasts up to 10 minutes. If a claim expires, another eligible participant may take a turn.

Notifications

STORIEE may show invitations and Story activity in the app and send push notifications when permitted by your device settings.

If notifications are missing, check your iPhone's notification settings and the in-app Activity screen.

Profile photographs

You may choose an optional profile photograph. Where a photograph cannot be shared under current access restrictions, other players may see your initials instead.

Purchases

STORIEE plans to offer optional one-time Plus and Unlimited upgrades. These are not subscriptions. Available purchases and prices will be displayed in the app when enabled.

Account deletion

Account deletion is different from signing out. Use the Me section to request deletion or email support if you cannot access your account.

Collaborative Story contributions already shared with other players may remain part of those Stories. See the Privacy Policy.

Reporting inappropriate content

Email support about harassment, threats, abusive content or other safety concerns. Include enough information to identify the problem, without sharing unnecessary personal data.

Age requirement

STORIEE is intended for players aged 16 and over.
