Contact & Support

A little help to keep the stories going.

Need a hand?

Whether a friend request is stuck, a Story won't load, or you've got a brilliant idea for YOUR GO, we'd love to hear from you.

What can we help with?

  • Signing in with Apple and accessing your account
  • YOUR GO IDs, friends and invitations
  • Creating, joining and reading Stories
  • Reporting bugs and technical problems
  • Reporting harassment or inappropriate content
  • Account deletion and privacy requests
  • Purchases and restoring purchases, when available

Reporting a problem

Please describe what happened, the screen you were using, and your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version if relevant. Your YOUR GO ID may also help us investigate.

Never send us passwords, verification codes or private Apple account details.

Reporting another player

YOUR GO should be a fun and welcoming place for friends to create stories together.

If you experience harassment, threats, offensive content or other inappropriate behaviour, contact us using the subject "YOUR GO Safety Report".

Include the Story title, relevant player ID and a description of the problem. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Account and privacy questions

You can contact us about accessing or deleting your personal information. Please also read our Privacy Policy.
