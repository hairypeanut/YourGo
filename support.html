Support & Help

Welcome to YOUR GO support. We're here to help you enjoy creating stories with your friends.

Contact Support

hairypeanut@mail.com

Please include a short description of the problem and, if helpful, your device model and iOS version. Never send passwords or authentication codes.

What is YOUR GO?

YOUR GO is a collaborative storytelling game where friends take turns writing a story together. Each contribution can contain up to 100 words. Then another player takes their turn.

Signing In

YOUR GO uses Sign in with Apple. You need to be signed in to access the game. If you sign out, you can sign back in using the same Apple account.

Friends & YOUR GO IDs

Every player receives a unique YOUR GO ID, which can be shared with friends so they can find and connect with you.

If you cannot find a friend or send a request, check that the YOUR GO ID has been entered correctly.

Stories & Contributions

Players can contribute up to 100 words per turn, with no daily word limit. A player cannot take two consecutive turns in the same Story.

Story writing uses a time-limited turn. If you encounter problems creating, joining or contributing to a Story, please contact support.

Profile Photographs

You can choose a profile photograph using your device. If uploading or displaying a photograph fails, check your internet connection and try again. Contact support if the problem continues.

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion from the Me section of the app. Deleting your account is different from signing out.

Account deletion is designed to remove your Firebase account, profile, YOUR GO ID, friend relationships and associated profile photograph. Local game data is also cleared as part of successful deletion, subject to technical limitations explained in our Privacy Policy.

If deletion fails or you cannot access your account, email hairypeanut@mail.com for assistance.

Reporting a Problem or Inappropriate Content

If you encounter abusive behaviour, threatening content, harassment or another safety concern, email support with the relevant details. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information.

Age Requirement

YOUR GO is intended for players aged 16 and over.
