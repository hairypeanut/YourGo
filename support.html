Contact & Support

A little help to keep the stories going.

Need a hand?

Whether a friend request is stuck, a Story won't load, or you've got a brilliant idea for YOUR GO, we'd love to hear from you.

Email YOUR GO Support

hairypeanut@mail.com

What can we help with?

  • Signing in with Apple and accessing your account
  • YOUR GO IDs, friends and invitations
  • Creating, joining and reading Stories
  • Reporting bugs and technical problems
  • Reporting harassment or inappropriate content
  • Account deletion and privacy requests
  • Purchases and restoring purchases, when available

Reporting a problem

Please describe what happened, which screen you were using, and your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version if relevant.

Your YOUR GO ID may also help us investigate.

Never send passwords, verification codes or private Apple account details.

Reporting another player

YOUR GO should be a fun and welcoming place for friends to create stories together.

If you experience harassment, threats, offensive content or inappropriate behaviour, please contact us.

Use the email subject YOUR GO Safety Report and include the relevant Story title, player ID and a description of the problem.

Avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Account deletion

If you wish to delete your YOUR GO account and associated personal information, use the account deletion option in the app where available, or email us.

Use the subject YOUR GO Account Deletion.

We may need to verify that you own the account before processing your request.

Privacy questions

You can contact us about accessing, correcting or deleting your personal information.

Please read our Privacy Policy for more information.
