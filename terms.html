4. How the game works

YOUR GO allows players to create collaborative Stories and invite friends to contribute.

The core writing rules are:

  • Each submitted contribution may contain 100 words or less.
  • The 100-word limit applies to each contribution, not to the entire day.
  • Players may contribute multiple times in a day when it is their turn.
  • A player cannot make two consecutive contributions to the same Story.
  • A writing claim may be limited to a 10-minute period.
  • Expired claims may be released so another eligible player can continue the Story.

We may refine gameplay rules as the app develops, including scoring, reactions and how Stories end.
