1. About STORIEE

Players take turns contributing to Stories together. Shared Stories are stored online between participants; older legacy Stories may be available only on a device.

2. Age requirement

STORIEE is intended for people aged 16 and over.

3. Accounts

STORIEE uses Sign in with Apple. You are responsible for access to your account and must not impersonate others, mislead players or attempt unauthorised access.

4. How Stories work

  • Each contribution may contain up to 100 words.
  • Players may contribute multiple times per day, but cannot make two consecutive contributions to the same Story.
  • A writing turn may be claimed for up to 10 minutes.
  • Shared Stories support 2–10 participants, including the creator.
  • Invitations may have registration windows of 15 minutes, 1 hour or 5 hours.
  • Accepted participants may begin playing before registration closes.
  • Some Story lifecycle decisions, including ending or archiving, are decided by player votes under the rules shown in the app.

5. Friends and invitations

Joining a shared Story may automatically create reciprocal friendships between participants. You can mark friends as favourites or remove a friendship.

Removing a friend does not remove them from existing Stories or delete previous contributions.
