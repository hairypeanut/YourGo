4. How the Game Works

YOUR GO is based on players taking turns writing a story together.

  • Each contribution must contain no more than 100 words.
  • The 100-word limit applies to each contribution, not to an entire day.
  • Players may contribute multiple times per day when it is their turn.
  • The same player cannot make two consecutive contributions to the same Story.
  • A writing turn may be claimed for up to 10 minutes.
  • Unsubmitted turns may expire when their claim period ends.
  • Other players may continue the Story when the turn becomes available.

Game mechanics may evolve as features are developed. Where changes materially affect gameplay, the app may explain the updated rules.
