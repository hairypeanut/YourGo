Our approach to privacy

YOUR GO is a collaborative storytelling game where friends take turns writing stories together.

We aim to collect only the information needed to provide the game, protect accounts and maintain a safe experience.

YOUR GO is not designed to sell personal information or use your private stories for advertising.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Account information: your authentication identifier, display name, YOUR GO ID and account settings.
  • Apple Sign-In information: the information Apple makes available when you choose to sign in, which may include a private relay email address.
  • Friends information: friend requests, accepted friendships and invitations.
  • Story information: story titles, contributions, participating players, timestamps and game activity.
  • Profile photographs: photographs you choose to upload, where this feature is available.
  • Technical information: information necessary for authentication, security, troubleshooting and reliable operation of the service.
  • Support correspondence: information you voluntarily send when contacting us.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure your account
  • Identify players through YOUR GO IDs
  • Manage friendships and invitations
  • Store and display collaborative stories
  • Coordinate player turns and game activity
  • Respond to support and safety reports
  • Prevent misuse and maintain service security
  • Meet applicable legal obligations

4. Apple Sign-In

YOUR GO uses Sign in with Apple for account authentication.

Apple may allow you to hide your personal email address using its private email relay service. YOUR GO does not require your Apple ID password.

5. Firebase and service providers

YOUR GO uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firebase Cloud Functions may process account, friendship and game operations.

Firebase Storage may be used for optional profile photographs where that feature is enabled.

These services may process and store information on infrastructure operated by Google. Processing locations and international transfers depend on the configured services and regions.

Learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security .

6. Who can see your Stories?

YOUR GO is designed around collaborative stories shared with participating players, rather than public social-media publishing.

Players participating in a Story can see its contributions and associated player information needed for the game.

Please avoid including sensitive personal details about yourself or others in Story contributions.

7. Photographs and camera access

If profile photographs are available, you may choose an image from your photo library or take a photograph using your camera.

YOUR GO should request access only when needed for the feature you choose to use.

You can manage camera and photo permissions through your device settings.

8. Purchases

YOUR GO plans to offer an optional one-time purchase unlocking unlimited active Story creation.

Where purchases are available, Apple handles payment processing. YOUR GO may receive transaction or entitlement information needed to unlock and restore purchases.

We do not need your full payment card details to provide this feature.

9. Legal bases for processing

Where UK data protection law applies, processing may rely on the performance of our agreement with you, legitimate interests in security and service operation, consent where required, or compliance with legal obligations.

The applicable basis depends on the information and the purpose of processing.

10. Data retention

We retain account and game information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide YOUR GO and meet applicable obligations.

Retention periods may differ for account records, shared Story contributions, security information and support correspondence.

Some records may need to be retained for legal or security reasons after an account is closed.

11. Deleting your account

Where available, you can request account deletion through the app. You can also contact support.

Account deletion is intended to remove your personal account information, YOUR GO ID, friendship records and associated profile data, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Contributions already added to a shared Story may need to remain so the other participants can continue reading their collaborative work. Personal identifiers should be removed or anonymised where appropriate.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or receive your personal information, and to object to certain processing.

You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

UK residents may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) .

13. Children's privacy

YOUR GO is not intended for children below the minimum age specified in the app's applicable eligibility rules.

If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to those rules, please contact support.

14. Security

We use authentication, access controls and other reasonable measures intended to protect personal information.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as YOUR GO develops. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.
