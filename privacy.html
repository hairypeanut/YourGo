Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

YOUR GO is a collaborative storytelling game developed and operated by Andrew Sadler, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom.

This Privacy Policy explains what information YOUR GO collects, how it is used and stored, and the choices available to you.

1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data?

Andrew Sadler is the data controller for personal information processed in connection with YOUR GO.

For privacy enquiries, contact hairypeanut@mail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, YOUR GO may process:

  • Account information: a unique Firebase account identifier and information provided through Sign in with Apple.
  • Profile information: your chosen display name, unique YOUR GO ID and optional profile photograph.
  • Friend information: friend requests, accepted friendships and the identifiers required to manage those connections.
  • Story information: Stories, contributions, player names and other game information stored locally on your device.
  • Technical information: information needed to authenticate users, maintain security, process requests and diagnose service failures.
  • Support correspondence: information you choose to include when contacting us.

Sign in with Apple may allow you to hide your personal email address. YOUR GO does not require you to disclose your personal email address to other players.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate and maintain your YOUR GO account.
  • Provide your player profile and unique YOUR GO ID.
  • Allow players to find and connect with friends.
  • Support game features and locally saved Stories.
  • Store and display an optional profile photograph.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate safety concerns.
  • Protect the service against misuse and unauthorised access.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Under applicable UK data protection law, we rely on appropriate legal bases depending on the activity:

  • Contract: processing needed to provide your account and the game features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: maintaining service security, preventing misuse and responding to operational problems, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligations: retaining or disclosing information where the law requires it.
  • Consent: where consent is specifically requested and required for an optional activity.

5. Where Your Information Is Stored

YOUR GO uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Firebase Storage.

Account and friend information is processed through Firebase. Optional profile photographs are stored in Firebase Storage.

Stories and contributions in the current version of YOUR GO are stored locally on the device rather than in a shared online Story database.

Firebase services may process information in different geographical regions, including outside the United Kingdom. YOUR GO's profile-photo Storage bucket is located in the United States. Where international transfers of personal information occur, applicable data protection safeguards are required.

6. Who Can See Your Information?

Your display name and YOUR GO ID are intended to help other players identify and connect with you. Friend-related information is used to manage the relevant player relationships.

Profile photographs are stored using account-specific access restrictions. The current Storage permissions restrict direct access to the authenticated account owner.

Locally stored Stories and contributions are available within the relevant signed-in account on that device. They are not currently synchronised as shared online Stories.

7. Third-Party Services

YOUR GO uses:

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and relevant iOS platform services.
  • Google Firebase: authentication, database, backend functions and profile-photo storage.

These providers process information as necessary to deliver their services and operate under their own applicable terms and privacy arrangements.

8. Data Retention

Account and profile information is generally retained while your account remains active and is subject to deletion when you delete your account.

Friend requests and friendship records are maintained as needed to provide those features and are included in the account-deletion process.

Local Stories and contributions remain in account-specific storage on the device until removed through the relevant game or account-deletion process.

Some limited information may be retained where required for legal obligations, security, dispute resolution or technical recovery. Service providers may also maintain backups according to their applicable retention practices.

9. Signing Out and Deleting Your Account

Signing out does not delete your account or its saved game data. Account-specific game data is intended to remain isolated so it cannot be accessed by a different signed-in account on the same device.

Deleting your account starts a process to remove your Firebase Authentication account, profile, unique YOUR GO ID reservation, friend requests, friendships and associated profile photograph.

The deletion process also removes the account's locally stored game file after the required remote steps succeed. If a deletion step fails, some information may remain until the operation is retried or resolved.

Older game data created before account-specific storage was introduced may remain in an unassigned local recovery file. That file is not automatically accessible to another signed-in account.

Deletion from active systems does not necessarily erase information immediately from backups, device snapshots or legally required records.

If you cannot complete deletion in the app, contact hairypeanut@mail.com.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of processing of your personal information. You may also have rights to object to certain processing and request data portability.

To exercise your rights, contact hairypeanut@mail.com.

You may also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Children and Young People

YOUR GO is intended for people aged 16 and over. People under 16 should not create an account or use the service.

If you believe someone under 16 has created an account, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

12. Security

YOUR GO uses Firebase Authentication, access-control rules and account-specific data storage to help protect personal information. No online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as YOUR GO develops. Material changes will be reflected in the published policy, with an updated date.
