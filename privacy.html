Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

STORIEE is a collaborative storytelling game developed and operated by Andrew Sadler, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Andrew Sadler is the data controller for personal information processed in connection with STORIEE. For privacy enquiries, email hairypeanut@mail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account: Firebase account identifier and information made available through Sign in with Apple, which may include an Apple private relay email address.
  • Profile: Display name, unique player ID and optional profile photograph.
  • Friends: Friendship connections, favourites, removals and related identifiers used to prevent removed friendships being recreated from older Stories.
  • Stories: Story titles, participants, invitations, registration deadlines, contributions, turn claims, votes and other gameplay records.
  • Notifications: Activity events and device messaging tokens needed to deliver opted-in notifications.
  • Technical and support: Authentication, security and diagnostic information and details sent when requesting support.

STORIEE does not require players to disclose their personal email addresses to other players.

3. How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts; display profiles; manage friendships, invitations, favourites and Story participation; save collaborative writing; operate turns and votes; deliver activity and notifications; respond to support requests; prevent misuse; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal bases

Depending on the processing activity, we rely on performance of a contract to provide the game, legitimate interests in security and reliable operation, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required for an optional feature.

5. Where information is stored

STORIEE uses Google Firebase, including Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase Storage and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Shared Stories and contributions are stored online so participating players can access them on their own devices. Some older legacy Stories may remain stored locally and may not be synchronised. Optional profile photographs are stored in Firebase Storage.

Firebase services may process data outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Applicable safeguards are required for international transfers of personal information.

6. Who can see your information?

Players in the same Story can see the shared Story, contributions and participant names necessary to play together. Friends can see information needed to identify one another. Favourite selections are private.

Profile-photo access may be limited by storage permissions, in which case initials may be displayed. Shared contributions may remain visible to Story participants after a friendship is removed.

7. Third-party services

Apple provides Sign in with Apple and iOS notification services. Google Firebase provides authentication, database, backend, storage and messaging services. Their processing is also governed by their applicable terms and privacy information.

8. Retention and deletion

Account and profile data are generally retained while an account remains active. Friendships and invitations are retained as needed for game features and integrity.

When account deletion is requested, the deletion process is intended to remove the account and associated personal records, subject to technical limitations, legal obligations and backup retention.

Contributions already shared with other participants may need to remain as part of the shared Story, or be anonymised where appropriate. Account deletion does not guarantee that every shared passage disappears from other participants' Stories or backups.

Signing out alone does not delete an account or Stories.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable UK data protection law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where relevant.

Contact hairypeanut@mail.com. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

10. Children

STORIEE is intended for people aged 16 and over.

11. Security

We use authentication and access-control mechanisms to protect data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as STORIEE evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised date.
